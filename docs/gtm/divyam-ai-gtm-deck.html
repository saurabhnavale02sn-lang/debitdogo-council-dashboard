Blockers & Objection Handling05 / 08

Two blockers kill this deal. Both have architectural answers.

01 · “You're unfunded. Not in my production path.”

  • Don't argue the balance sheet. Remove the dependency.
  • Lead with air-gapped deployment — router and judge inside their VPC. If Divyam disappears, their inference doesn't.
  • One workload, 30-day paid pilot, success = ₹ saved at constant quality. Monthly terms.
  • Same answer unlocks BFSI. One architecture, two blockers cleared.

02 · “We already use Portkey or LangChain.”

  • Never attack the gateway. “Keep it — that's plumbing. I sell the decision layer above it.”
  • The answer is no everywhere: a human authors the routing rule. That gap is the sale.
  • Pre-empt “won't OpenAI build this?” — no provider gets paid to route you off its own model. Neutrality is the product.
The disqualifying question: “Does your gateway benchmark DeepSeek against your production data overnight and switch traffic by morning — without an engineer approving it?”

GTM Motion — Days 1–3007 / 08

Two engines, deliberately unequal.

Outbound · 80% of my time · delivers Q1 revenue

  • 50 accounts, ~200 contacts, hand-verified. Personas: VP Platform Eng, AI Infra Lead, CTO.
  • 5 touches / 12 days. Every message carries one falsifiable claim about their stack.
  • Lead line: “Cut your OpenAI inference bill by 40% this week without breaking your application.”
  • Disqualify fast: no AI in prod, under ~$10k/mo inference, or no exec sponsor by call two.

Inbound · 20% of my time · compounds from Q2

  • Free inference cost audit — 30-min teardown returned as a modelled savings estimate. Highest-intent magnet.
  • Quarterly benchmark reports. In DevTools, benchmarks are distribution — engineers forward them internally.
  • OSS cost calculator + comparison SEO (“Portkey alternative”). Zero CAC.
  • Founder-led posts. Your voice travels further than any brand account we could build.
