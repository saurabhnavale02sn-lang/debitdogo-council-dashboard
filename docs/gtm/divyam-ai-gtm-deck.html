Market Reality — Geography04 / 18

Two markets. Two entirely different buyers.

United States

  • Heavily funded, converging on broad AI observability — traces, spans, scores, dashboards.
  • Braintrust raised one of 2026's largest eval rounds; LangSmith owns the LangChain-native install base.
  • Buyer: the AI engineer debugging quality. Budget: tooling line.

India

  • Hyper cost-conscious. Buys ROI inside a quarter, not a platform vision inside a year.
  • Sovereignty is contractual. DPDP plus RBI payment-data localisation gate every BFSI conversation before feature one.
  • DPDP programmes alone run ₹15–60 lakh at large enterprises — we compete for that same budget line.
  • Buyer: the executive defending margin. Budget: cost of goods.
Consequence for our roadmap: air-gapped deployment is not a feature. It is the entry ticket to the top 50 accounts in this market.

Objection Handling — 0109 / 18

“You're unfunded. I can't put you in the production path.”

The most common killer in enterprise DevTools, and the one we can architect away.
  • Do not argue the balance sheet. Remove the dependency.
  • Lead with air-gapped, privately hosted deployment: the router and the ~8B judge run inside their VPC. No traffic leaves their perimeter. Divyam disappearing tomorrow does not take their inference down.
  • De-risk commercially: one workload, 30-day paid pilot, success defined as a number — ₹ saved at constant quality. Monthly terms. Written exit clause.
  • Then reframe the purchase: “You are not betting your infrastructure on us. You are renting an optimisation loop that runs on your own metal.”
Secondary benefit: the same air-gapped posture is what unlocks BFSI. One architectural answer clears two blockers.

GTM Motion — Architecture12 / 18

Two engines, deliberately unequal.

Outbound buys us revenue this quarter. Inbound buys us leverage next quarter. Confusing the two is how bootstrapped startups die.

Engine 1 · Outbound — 80% of my time

  • Named-account, founder-assisted, technically led. 50 accounts, ~200 contacts, hand-verified.
  • Produces all three logos in the first 90 days.
  • Cost: my time plus ~₹40k/month of tooling. No media spend.
  • Fails if: I template it. Personalisation is the channel.

Engine 2 · Inbound — 20% of my time

  • Technical credibility compounding into demand. Benchmarks, teardowns, a free audit.
  • Produces near-zero pipeline in Q1 — and half of it by Q3. I will not pretend otherwise.
  • Cost: content time. Distribution is borrowed, not bought.
  • Fails if: we write marketing copy. Engineers forward data; they delete decks.
The honest trade: if I spend Q1 building inbound, we have beautiful content and no revenue. Outbound first is a sequencing decision, not a preference.

Engine 1 — Outbound Motion13 / 18

Five touches, twelve days, one falsifiable claim each.

The sequence

  • D1 · Email — one specific observation about their AI feature + the cost thesis. No calendar link.
  • D3 · LinkedIn — connect with the champion. No pitch attached.
  • D5 · Email — the MakeMyTrip 63% teardown as an artifact.
  • D8 · Email — the disqualifying question, sent cold.
  • D12 · Break-up — cost calculator link, permission to close the file.

Message by persona

  • VP Platform Eng — “Here is your monthly inference bill, modelled.” Economic pain.
  • AI Infra Lead — “Here is how we prove quality holds before traffic moves.” Technical risk.
  • CTO — “Here is the margin story for your board.” Strategic cover.
  • Never send the same artifact to two personas in one account.

Rules of engagement

  • Tier 1 (15 accounts): fully hand-written, plus a modelled cost estimate before first reply.
  • Tier 2 (35 accounts): segment-templated, still manually verified.
  • Disqualify fast: no AI in production, or under ~$10k/month inference, or no exec sponsor by call two.
  • Volume: 50 accounts and ~200 contacts worked per month, ~1,000 touches.

Execution — 90 Days16 / 18

Map, then hunt, then systematise.

Days 1 – 30 · Build & Launch
Earn the right to be technical
  • Map three personas: VP Platform Engineering (economic), AI Infra Lead (champion), CTO (air cover).
  • 50 accounts → ~200 verified contacts. No purchased lists.
  • Stack live: warmed inboxes, Sales Navigator, one sequencer, one CRM.
  • Launch lead line: “Cut your OpenAI inference bill by 40% this week without breaking your application.”
  • Ship inbound play 1: the free cost audit.
Days 31 – 60 · Convert
Turn interest into invoices
  • Run pilots: one workload, success criteria signed before kickoff.
  • Measure in ₹ saved at constant quality — their finance team's units, not ours.
  • First benchmark report published; comparison pages live.
  • Tighten ICP on evidence: double down on whichever segment replies, cut the rest.
Days 61 – 90 · Systematise
Make it repeatable without me
  • 3 closed logos and one public case study cleared for use.
  • POC runbook written so the next AE executes it unaided.
  • Pricing validated against three real negotiations, not a spreadsheet.
  • Inbound reporting its first sourced pipeline.
