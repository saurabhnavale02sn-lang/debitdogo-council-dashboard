Market Reality

Two markets. Two entirely different buyers.

United States

  • Heavily funded, converging on broad AI observability — traces, spans, scores, dashboards.
  • Braintrust raised one of 2026's largest eval rounds. LangSmith owns the LangChain install base.
  • Their buyer is the AI engineer debugging quality.

India

  • Hyper cost-conscious. ROI in a quarter, not a platform vision in a year.
  • Sovereignty is contractual, not aspirational. DPDP + RBI payment-data localisation gate every BFSI deal.
  • DPDP programmes alone run ₹15–60 lakh at large enterprises — we compete against that budget.
  • Our buyer is the exec defending margin.
→ Consequence: air-gapped deployment is not a feature. It is the entry ticket to the top 50 accounts in this market.

The Bootstrapped Reality

Stated plainly, so we don't pretend otherwise.

  • We will lose every paid-search, conference-booth and content-volume fight. Portkey has 15 million reasons to win those.
  • We win on architecture and on reps. 100 surgical, technically-literate touches beat 100,000 impressions.
  • Our unfair advantages are exactly three: the closed loop, air-gapped deployment, and a POC shipped the same week it is scoped.
  • Corollary — and I mean this as a commitment: zero top-of-funnel spend in the first 90 days. Every rupee goes to data, tooling and POC engineering.

Blocker 01

“You’re unfunded. I can’t put you in the production path.”

  • Do not argue the balance sheet. Remove the dependency.
  • Lead with air-gapped / privately hosted deployment: router and the ~8B judge run inside their VPC. No traffic leaves. Divyam going away tomorrow does not take their inference down.
  • De-risk commercially: one workload, 30-day paid pilot, success = a number (₹ saved at constant quality), monthly terms, exit clause.
  • Reframe: “You are not betting your infra on us. You are renting an optimisation loop that runs on your own metal.”

Blocker 02

“We already use Portkey / LangChain.”

  • Never attack the gateway. Concede it and reframe the category: “Good — keep it. That’s your plumbing. I’m selling the decision layer above it.”

Execution · Days 1–30

Map. Build. Hit the phones.

Personas

  • VP / Head of Platform Eng — owns the bill, feels it monthly. Economic buyer.
  • AI Infra / ML Platform Lead — owns router and risk. Technical champion; wins or kills it.
  • CTO — owns the margin story to the board. Air cover and signature.

Build · Week 1–2

  • 50 accounts → ~200 named contacts, hand-verified. No purchased lists.
  • Cheap stack: inbox warm-up, Sales Nav, one sequencer, CRM. Zero paid media.
  • Weaponise existing assets: MakeMyTrip 63%, the model inertia post. Engineers forward technical content; they delete decks.

Outbound · Week 2–4

  • Lead line: “Cut your OpenAI inference bill by 40% this week without breaking your application.”
  • Every message carries one falsifiable technical observation about their stack. No “quick chat.”
  • 5 touches / 12 days. 40 accounts and 200 contacts worked per week.
